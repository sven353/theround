The operating system

A Year-Round Cadence Built for Leaders at Scale

Leading a high-velocity business is cognitively isolating. The Round is an integrated leadership architecture designed to prevent strategic drift, dissolve operational fog, and enforce execution rigor.

Monthly

The Executive Peer Board

Confidential, closed-door working sessions with a curated cohort of founders and CEOs. No polite updates or passive socializing: live strategic dilemmas, real-time stress testing, and decisive peer feedback.

  • Strict facilitator governance, zero advice in disguise
  • Facilitation protocols adapted to current challenges

Monthly

Strategic Sparring (1:1 & Pods)

Dedicated executive sparring between sessions. Conducted 1:1 with your facilitator, or organized into focused 1:2 / 1:3 pods when members navigate shared inflections such as M&A integration, founder transitions or restructuring.

  • Unfiltered sounding board on high-friction calls
  • Cross-pollination on shared bottlenecks

Annual milestone

The 24-Hour Immersion

One night and one full day in a secluded location. Designed to break operational inertia, revisit foundational leadership models, and conduct deep personal and strategic alignment away from Slack and meetings.

  • Structured introspective exercises and peer dinners
  • Personal and business recalibration

Annual milestone

The Executive Retreat & Offsite

A multi-day, immersive offsite in nature. Combines high-level market perspective, executive health, systemic alignment, and deep peer bonding in an environment built for clarity.

  • Curated venue chosen for mental bandwidth
  • Long-range vision mapping and governance review

Always-on

The Member Accountability Ledger

A private digital infrastructure. Commitments made in the room do not evaporate. Each hard call is tracked with explicit execution deadlines, peer accountability partners, and measured outcome ROI.

  • Milestone tracking and partner check-ins
  • Searchable archive of cohort case studies and hard calls

Facilitation technology

A Toolkit That Evolves With the Challenge

Every business challenge requires a distinct diagnostic lens. We deploy rotating protocols and modern intelligence layers throughout the year.

  1. 01

    Dynamic Facilitation Protocols

    Rotating advisory structures: 40-minute rapid-fire issue processing, governance stress-tests, silent deliberation rounds, and systemic bottleneck diagnostics.

  2. 02

    AI-Enhanced Dilemma Framing

    Pre-session context extraction to isolate the real tension, calculate the 90-day cost of inaction, and present clean Option A versus Option B dilemmas with zero fluff.

  3. 03

    The Hard Call Archive

    A structured repository of executed decisions, trade-offs, and aftermath metrics across the cohort, giving members proven playbook data from peer leaders.

  4. 04

    Closed-Loop Accountability

    No decision is left open-ended. Every session concludes with an explicit compact: a named action, a non-negotiable target date, and an assigned peer check-in partner.

Inside one protocol

The 40-Minute Speed Board

The sharpest tool in the kit for a time-bound call. Five phases, one protagonist, one decision.

  1. 1

    Context & Framing

    2–3 facts, Option A versus B, the 90-day cost of inaction.

  2. 2

    Clarifying

    Questions only. No opinions, no disguised advice.

  3. 3

    Deliberation

    The board debates trade-offs. The protagonist listens and takes notes.

  4. 4

    Hard Call Round

    30–45 seconds each: decide [X], execute by [Y], accept [Z].

  5. 5

    Commitment

    Decision, deadline and partner, logged to the ledger.
