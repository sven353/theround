The operating architecture

A year-round cadence built for leaders at scale

Leading a high-velocity business is cognitively isolating. The Round combines five formats into one operating rhythm, so strategic thinking keeps pace with execution.

Monthly

The Executive Peer Board

In-person, closed-door working sessions with a curated cohort of founders and CEOs. High-density strategic sparring on live dilemmas, held under strict Chatham House confidentiality.

  • Facilitator-governed, zero unsolicited advice
  • Protocol chosen to fit the dilemma on the table

Monthly

Strategic Sparring (1:1 & Pods)

Direct executive sparring with the facilitator between boards, plus curated 1:2 or 1:3 pods for leaders facing identical friction points such as founder transitions, M&A integrations or restructuring.

  • A private sounding board for high-friction calls
  • Pods formed around shared inflection points

Annual

The 24-Hour Immersion

A secluded overnight retreat, one night and one full day, designed to break operational inertia and recalibrate your foundational trajectory away from operational noise.

  • Structured reflection and peer dinners
  • Personal and business recalibration

Annual

The Executive Offsite & Sanctuary

A multi-day retreat in nature combining long-range vision mapping, executive stamina, systemic alignment and deep peer bonding.

  • Venue chosen for mental bandwidth
  • Vision mapping and governance review

Continuous

The Member Accountability Ledger

A digital operating record that keeps commitments made in the room from evaporating. Explicit deadlines, assigned peer sparring partners and measured outcome ROI for every hard call.

  • Deadline tracking and partner check-ins
  • Private to members of the cohort

The In-Room & Retreat Environment · Closed-Door Deliberations

Intimate physical spaces designed for deep strategic focus, high candor, and zero operational distraction.

The dynamic facilitation toolkit

Forty-minute issue processing is one mode among several

Every challenge calls for its own diagnostic lens. The facilitator rotates protocols across the year and adds an intelligence layer before and after each session.

  1. 01

    Adaptive Facilitation Protocols

    Rotating structures between rapid-fire 40-minute issue processing, systemic root-cause debriefs and silent deliberation rounds.

  2. 02

    AI-Assisted Context Framing

    Pre-session synthesis that isolates the operational baseline, calculates the 90-day cost of inaction and surfaces hidden blind spots.

  3. 03

    The Hard Call Archive

    An anonymised cohort decision database capturing trade-offs, playbooks and aftermath metrics from experienced operators.

  4. 04

    Closed-Loop Accountability

    A structured compact at the end of every session: a named decision, a non-negotiable target date and an assigned peer check-in partner.

Retreats & sanctuary

Step out of execution to sharpen your trajectory.

Daily operations produce a steady cognitive fog: back-to-back meetings, reactive decisions and very little time to see the whole system. Twice a year, The Round removes members from that environment on purpose, so the slower thinking that shapes the next year can actually happen.

Member caliber & curation

Who sits at The Round

A peer table is only as valuable as the candor and commitment of the people around it. Every cohort is curated to protect that standard.

The standard

Who belongs

Founders, CEOs & Managing Directors
Leading organisations of 5 to 2,000 people.
International perspective
Operating across borders, cultures or rapid organisational change.
Radical candor & emotional maturity
Able to put real tension on the table and hear hard feedback.
Principled tension
Willing to challenge peers firmly and hold mutual accountability.

Out of scope

Who we do not admit

Sales & BD prospecting
Anyone using the cohort as a pipeline.
Passive spectators
Leaders who attend without contributing to deliberation.
Pre-PMF ventures
Companies where execution bottlenecks have not yet emerged.
Advice-givers
People who prescribe quickly without deep inquiry first.
