The Round BCN1 is a confidential, year-round executive peer advisory board facilitated by Dr. Sven Mulfinger and operated by Kuma Partners. The cohort meets in person once every month in the Barcelona area for a dedicated six-hour working session. The annual cadence comprises 10 monthly Board sessions, one 24-Hour Immersion overnight retreat and one 3- to 4-day Executive Offsite & Sanctuary in nature, alongside monthly 55-minute 1:1 strategic sparring sessions.

Membership is curated strictly for active Founders, Chief Executive Officers (CEOs) and Managing Directors who carry ultimate P&L and strategic responsibility for scaling or established enterprises. Every cohort is strictly capped at 8 to 12 members to ensure psychological safety, intimacy and high-density deliberations.

Direct competitors are never admitted to the same cohort. Before any candidate is admitted, existing members review their company profile and sector to raise potential conflicts. We operate under strict Chatham House confidentiality: what is shared in the room never leaves the room, extending to members' boards, co-founders and personal lives.

The Round eliminates open-ended, rigid 40-minute issue processing and generic keynote speakers. Delivered directly by senior former operators and backed by the Kuma Operating System (KOS), it uses adaptive facilitation protocols such as silent deliberation, rapid-fire pressure testing and root-cause diagnostics. Commitments are formally captured in a digital Accountability Ledger with assigned peer check-in partners to measure real execution velocity.

Sessions are held once a month in the Barcelona area. Members take turns hosting the cohort at their respective corporate headquarters or private offices. Hosting at each other's offices provides tangible operational context that sharpens peer feedback. The host provides a private, secure meeting room with breakfast and lunch.

The Round incorporates two annual retreats that replace the monthly Board session during those cycles: a 24-Hour Immersion (one night, one full day) and the Annual Executive Offsite & Sanctuary (3 to 4 days secluded in nature). Full programmatic architecture and operator facilitation are included in the membership fee. Direct travel, accommodation and meal expenses are covered individually by each member at cost.

Human presence and authentic interpersonal trust take priority. Sessions are never recorded, and AI note-taking bots are prohibited in the room. While Kuma Partners and members may use AI models for anonymised pre-session context preparation or strategic synthesis, all input data is strictly anonymised with names, companies and identifiers removed. Data stored in the Member Accountability Ledger is deleted within 30 days of membership termination.

Peer collaborations, joint ventures, advisory relationships and commercial pilots are permitted, provided there is complete transparency. Members must disclose bilateral collaborations to the facilitator so the cohort understands shared interests. Unsolicited pitching, service selling, fundraising or employee poaching inside the cohort is strictly prohibited.

Membership operates in rolling six-month cycles at €3,000 plus VAT per cycle, billed in advance. A one-time admission fee of €500 is waived for Founding Members joining Barcelona 01 by 15 October 2026. A one-time admission fee of €500 is payable on admission. Membership continues automatically every six months and can be terminated by providing written notice at least 30 days prior to the end of the current period.

Caliber and psychological safety rely on consistent attendance. Members commit to attending every monthly Board session and retreat on time for the full duration. If an executive misses more than three sessions in a calendar year, a formal review is conducted with the facilitator to evaluate cohort continuity.
