The operating architecture

A year-round cadence built for leaders at scale

Five formats combine into one operating rhythm, so strategic thinking keeps pace with execution.

Monthly

The Executive Peer Board

A 6-hour in-person working session held monthly in the Barcelona area, strictly capped at 8 to 12 vetted members.

  • Members take turns hosting at their own companies and open with operational context
  • Breakfast and lunch provided by the host

Chatham House Rule · Zero Direct Competitors

Monthly

Strategic Sparring: 1:1 & Pods

A monthly 55-minute 1:1 sparring session with Dr. Sven Mulfinger between boards, to prepare live dilemmas and review commitments.

  • Rotates into 90-minute pods with 1–2 peers
  • Pods form around identical strategic or co-founder friction

Annual

The 24-Hour Immersion

A secluded overnight retreat, one night and one full day, designed to break operational inertia away from day-to-day noise.

  • Replaces the monthly board session for that cycle

Program & full facilitation included in membership fee

Annual

The Annual Executive Offsite & Sanctuary

A 3- to 4-day deep retreat in nature combining vision mapping, systemic alignment and deep peer bonding.

  • Replaces the monthly board session for that cycle

Program & full facilitation included in membership fee

Continuous

The Member Accountability Ledger

A private digital operating record tracking named decisions, explicit deadlines and assigned peer check-in partners.

  • Zero evaporating promises
  • GDPR-compliant: member data is deleted within 30 days of leaving

Standards of fit · The Round Charter

Standards of fit and cohort integrity

A peer board is only as strong as the people around the table. Every seat is vetted against The Round Charter.

Ideal cohort fit

Who it is for

  • Active Founders, CEOs and Managing Directors carrying ultimate P&L and strategic responsibility
  • High candor, low ego
  • Willing to show up on time for every session

Not a fit

Who it is not for

  • Pure service providers
  • Transactional networkers
  • Unsolicited selling or pitching
  • Passive listeners

Cohort integrity

Cohort integrity

Direct competitor exclusivity
The existing cohort reviews and consents to every new member's company and sector.
Human-first discretion
No recordings, no passive AI bots and strict Chatham House Rule. AI is used solely for anonymised pre-session preparation.
